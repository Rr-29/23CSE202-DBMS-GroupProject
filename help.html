Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

Go to the Login page, click Forgot Password, verify your identity using the OTP, and create a new password.

How can I transfer money?

Open the Transfer page, choose your account, select a beneficiary, enter the amount, and confirm the transaction.

How do I add a beneficiary?

Visit the Beneficiaries page and click Add Beneficiary. Complete the required verification before transfers.

Can I block my debit card?

Yes. Go to the Cards page and use the Card Controls section to freeze or block your card instantly.
